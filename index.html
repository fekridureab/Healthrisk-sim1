Case 1 — Collapsed Supply Chain: Operational Risk in Action

Al-Rayyan Surgical Centre · 150-bed private hospital · Doha, Qatar · January 2026 · ASHRM ERM — Operational Risk Domain

Al-Rayyan Surgical Centre performs approximately 4,500 surgeries per year. The hospital relies on a single external supplier for ALL sterile surgical supplies — drapes, gowns, instrument packs, and irrigation fluids. The supplier contract was last reviewed in 2021.

On January 15, 2026, your team — the Risk Management Committee — receives an urgent call from the supplier: their manufacturing facility has been damaged by flooding in Southeast Asia. Deliveries are suspended for 6–8 weeks. You have only 4 days of sterile supply stock remaining. 23 elective surgeries are scheduled for next week. The CEO has not been informed yet.

Key background: The risk register did NOT include supply chain dependency as a risk before this event. The supplier had one previous delivery failure in 2023 — no corrective action was taken. One patient (Mr. Al-Rashidi, 58) is waiting for urgent cancer surgery on January 20.
